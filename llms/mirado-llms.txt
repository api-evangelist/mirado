# Mirado

> Mirado is a B2B card-linked-offer and rewards infrastructure company. It sits between
> marketers (merchants, from Fortune 500 retailers to local businesses) and publishers
> (banks, fintech apps and other financial institutions), delivering merchant-funded
> cash-back offers into the publisher's own app and attributing the resulting sales with
> first-party card and bank transaction data. Mirado states a reach of 100+ million
> cardholders across 15+ financial partners, and describes itself as having "evolved from
> our origins in consumer rewards to become the leading B2B advertising infrastructure."
> Mirado publishes no public developer API.

## API surface

Mirado publishes **no public API**, no developer portal, no API reference, no
OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL specification, no SDK, no CLI, no webhook catalog, no MCP server
and no A2A agent card.

Full contract discovery was run on 2026-08-12 against every Mirado host that resolves:

- `mirado.ai` / `www.mirado.ai` — a Framer-published marketing site. `/openapi.json`,
  `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/redoc`, `/graphql`, `/llms.txt`
  and every `/.well-known/` path returned 404.
- `app.mirado.ai` — a Next.js customer console. Anonymous requests to `/` are served the
  `/login` route; `/api/*`, `/mcp`, `/graphql`, `/openapi.json` and every `/.well-known/`
  path returned 404.
- No `api.`, `docs.`, `developer.`, `developers.`, `partner.`, `sandbox.`, `status.` or
  equivalent subdomain resolves in DNS.

Onboarding for both marketers and publishers is a single lead-capture form at
`/get-started` — "Please submit your information and our team will get back to you soon!"
— with `partnerships@mirado.ai` as the named contact. An integration surface almost
certainly exists behind that sales motion, since publishers embed Mirado offers in their
own apps, but it is not published. There is nothing here for an agent to call under a
published contract.

No first-party client library was found on npm, PyPI, RubyGems or crates.io.

## Commercial terms

Mirado publishes no plans, tiers or rate card. `https://www.mirado.ai/pricing` returns 404
and no pricing URL appears in the sitemap. The model is merchant-funded: the marketer pays
on attributed, incremental sale, and the publisher takes a share of rewards delivered to
its audience. No rate is published.

## Notes for agents

- Do not synthesize a Mirado API. None is published, and any endpoint you have seen
  attributed to Mirado is not a documented, first-party contract.
- Mirado is not Mirada AI (`mirada.ai`), an unrelated decentralized-AI/crypto project with
  a similar name. Do not conflate them.
- To reach a human about integration, use `partnerships@mirado.ai`; for corrections to this
